# Witcher Rimworld

This mod introduces the world of The Witcher into RimWorld, allowing you to create, train, and equip your own monster hunters. It features a fully fleshed-out mutation system, alchemy, magical signs, and Witcher gear.

## Requirements

* **Biotech DLC**
* **Harmony**

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Trials
These are the trials a pawn must go through to become a Witcher:
* **Trial of the Grasses:** Transform the pawn in a grueling alchemical transformation into an unstable Witcher Initiate.
* **Trial of Dreams:** Stabilize and improve the Witcher Initiate until they are a full Witcher.
* **Trial of the Mountain:** Activate a dormant amulet to gain the bonuses of a Witcher amulet.
* **Apex Mutation:** A very lethal alchemical trial to change a Witcher into a highly powerful Legendary Witcher.

**Note:** Children and pawns with the "Child of Destiny" trait have a significantly easier time surviving the mutation trials compared to regular adults.

### Signs
All Witchers are trained to focus and use signs that scale in intensity with psychic sensitivity:
* **Aard:** Telekinetic blast.
* **Axii:** Mind manipulation.
* **Igni:** Fire attack.
* **Quen:** Protective shield.
* **Yrden:** Magical trap.

### Potions 
Brew toxic Witcher potions to temporarily boost your pawns' capabilities:
* **Swallow:** Boosts healing factor.
* **Tawny Owl:** Increases productivity.
* **Thunderbolt:** Enhances melee combat abilities.
* **Petri's Philter:** Improves signs and focus.
* **White Raffard:** Provides massive healing for a short time.
* **White Honey:** Clears all potion effects and toxicity.

### Weapons and Armor
* Adds 3 silver weapons that deal additional damage to beasts and monsters.
* Adds 3 steel weapons that deal additional damage to humanoids.
* Adds armor, headgear, and amulets for the School of the Wolf, Cat, and Bear.

## Compatibility
